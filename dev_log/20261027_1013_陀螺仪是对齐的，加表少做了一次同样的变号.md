$$
\delta x=\begin{bmatrix}\delta\phi^b_{3}&\delta v^s_{3}&\delta p^s_{3}&\delta b_a^b_{3}&\delta b_g^b_{3}&\delta s_{5}\end{bmatrix}^{\mathrm{T}}
$$

$\delta\phi^b$ 是 $C_b^s$ 的右扰动。$C_b^s=(C_s^e)^{\mathrm{T}}C_b^e$，$f^b$ 是已经减去加表零偏后的比力。连续方程是

$$
\begin{aligned}
\delta\dot\phi^b&=-\delta b_g\\
\delta\dot v^s&=-C_b^s[f^b]_{\times}\delta\phi^b+C_b^s\delta b_a\\
\delta\dot p^s&=\delta v^s\\
\delta\dot b_a&=0,\qquad \delta\dot b_g=0,\qquad \delta\dot s=0
\end{aligned}
$$

离散成 $\Phi\approx I+F\Delta t$，非零的非对角块只有

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Phi_{\phi,b_g}&=-I\Delta t\\
\Phi_{v,\phi}&=-C_b^s[f^b]_{\times}\Delta t\\
\Phi_{v,b_a}&=C_b^s\Delta t\\
\Phi_{p,v}&=I\Delta t
\end{aligned}
$$

协方差更新是

$$
P\leftarrow \Phi P\Phi^{\mathrm{T}}+Q
$$

$Q$ 只有对角元。姿态是角随机游走
