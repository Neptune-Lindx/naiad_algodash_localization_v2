预测速度用 INS 的 \(\hat z_i=(1+s_{k_i})\,v_{\mathrm{rel},x}^b\)。每个轮子先用还没放大的方差算标准化残差

\tilde v_i=\frac{|z_i-\hat z_i|}{\sqrt{Q_{ii}}}
\]

\[
\lambda_i=
\begin{cases}
1, & \tilde v_i\le k_0\\
\dfrac{\tilde v_i}{k_0}\left(\dfrac{k_1-k_0}{k_1-\tilde v_i}\right)^2, & k_0<\tilde v_i<k_1\\
10^6, & \tilde v_i\ge k_1
\end{cases}
\]
